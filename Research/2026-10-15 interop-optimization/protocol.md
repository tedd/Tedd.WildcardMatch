Starting revision: `7574a36`, clean `main`; integration branch `main`.

Objective: improve the production direct matcher against a fresh native baseline with optimized interop where qualified. A clear lead means at least 20% lower mean cost than the fastest competitor per original workload. Primary API: reused Tedd direct matcher. Static API is reported separately and must not regress by more than 5% against its fresh control. A result below the target is reported as such; timings, fixtures, native work and competitor implementations remain observable and unchanged.

Four original fixtures and all existing managed/native competitors remain fixed. Unqualified/long native calls retain transitions. No presumed interop overhead is subtracted. Encoding and preparation are excluded consistently with the previous throughput comparison; lifetime and UTF-16 versus byte representation remain explicit limits.

Method: DotNetLab controlled experiments and diagnostics through the supported CLI in a fresh local catalog. Hold `Global\ScientificMethodPerformanceHostLock` through builds, tests and measurements. Screening: one process, ten randomized paired blocks, fixed seed 20261015, two warmups, 300,000 matches per short block and 30,000 per long block; descriptive evidence only. Inspect emitted code or diagnostic stacks before attributing a mechanism. Preserve all tested candidates and reasons for rejection. Parallelism, offload and persistent result caching are inapplicable to this synchronous, per-string API.

Correctness guardrails: preserve full existing string/span, newline/final-LF, embedded-NUL, Unicode, option/culture, timeout, boundary and randomized contracts across all target frameworks. Exercise new strategy boundaries with an independent oracle. Retain long-input, dense-anchor and newline regression workloads from prior research. Zero additional per-match allocation.

Confirmation: freeze the selected source before new confirmation data; fresh normal builds and ten paired process sessions per relevant control/candidate scenario, no failed-session replacement, sample expansion or favorable-run selection. Fresh processes reset code/state but do not establish independence from host load or thermal drift. Report assumptions and observer limits. Require improvement of the targeted path and at most 5% regression on mandatory guardrails. Before confirmation execution, broaden the 0.05 family error budget to all 17 comparisons: 13 reused scenarios (including an equal-row portfolio of the original four fixtures and broader pattern shapes) and the original four static scenarios. Use 5% useful-improvement and equivalence boundaries, with a 5% regression limit. External native comparisons and runtimes outside DotNetLab's qualified promotion lane use separately identified BenchmarkDotNet evidence: three launches, three warmups, ten 100 ms iterations, processor 30 affinity and tiering disabled.

Initial hypotheses, before candidate measurements:

1. Short-star bitset recurrence performs avoidable per-character work versus a compact greedy or prepared segment matcher. Compare routing and scalar/packed alternatives without sacrificing newline semantics.
2. Accepting a completed trailing star early can reduce scanning, but must still reject interior LF outside Singleline. Compare an explicit remainder check; reject if its overhead or semantics regress.
3. Static pattern classification and non-inlined generic dispatch account for part of its short-input cost. Inspect generated code and compare a bounded combined classifier or hot-path split.
4. Native normal transitions distort per-match latency for sub-microsecond functions. Compare normal and suppressed call sites plus a trivial native control; use actual native batches for the throughput floor.
